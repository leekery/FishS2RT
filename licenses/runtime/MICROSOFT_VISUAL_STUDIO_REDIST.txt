The Windows runtime wheel copies MSVCP140.dll, VCRUNTIME140.dll,
VCRUNTIME140_1.dll, and VCOMP140.DLL from the installed Visual Studio 2022
VC\Redist\MSVC\<version>\x64 directories.

Microsoft's current redistribution guidance and the applicable Visual Studio
license terms govern those files:
https://learn.microsoft.com/cpp/windows/redistributing-visual-cpp-files

The DLLs themselves are not committed to the FishS2RT source repository.
